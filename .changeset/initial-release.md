First release of the Tauri SDK: `tauri-plugin-licensemanager` (crate) and `@licenseman/tauri` (JS bindings), a port of `@licenseman/electron` with compatible storage and fingerprint. Validations send a `User-Agent` naming the SDK, its version, and the Tauri version and OS (`licensemanager-tauri/1.0.0 (tauri/2.12.0; rust; macos aarch64)`).
